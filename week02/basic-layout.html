President Dallin H. Oaks

18th President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Dallin H. Oaks has dedicated his life to faith, education, leadership, and service. He became the 18th President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints on October 14, 2025.

A Life of Service and Faith

President Oaks was born on August 12, 1932, in Provo, Utah. He studied at Brigham Young University and the University of Chicago Law School. Throughout his career, he worked as an attorney, educator, university president, and judge.
